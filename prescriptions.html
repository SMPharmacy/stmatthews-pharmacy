Prescriptions

Nominate us once and your NHS prescriptions come to us automatically — no paper, no queueing at the surgery. Then we'll get them to you, free, wherever you are locally.

EPS Nomination

Nominate us as your pharmacy

The Electronic Prescription Service (EPS) sends your prescription straight from your GP to whichever pharmacy you've nominated. It's the NHS default now, and it means no paper slip to lose and nothing to hand in.

Fill in the form and we'll set the nomination up for you — it takes about a minute, you only ever do it once, and you can change it whenever you like. It doesn't lock you in, and you can still use any other pharmacy any time you want.

Repeat Prescriptions

Repeats, handled

If you take the same medicines every month, you shouldn't have to think about it. Once you've nominated us, we can manage the whole repeat cycle: we'll order from your surgery, chase them if they're slow, and let you know the moment it's ready.

We'll also keep an eye on the things that get missed — a review that's overdue, an item that's quietly dropped off your list, a medicine that's about to run out while you're away. That's the part a chain won't do for you.

  • We order your repeats from the surgery for you
  • A text or call when your prescription is ready
  • We'll flag overdue medication reviews before they bite
  • Holiday supplies sorted in advance — just give us notice

Free Service

Free collection & delivery

We collect your prescription from your GP surgery and deliver it to your door. It's free, it's local, and there's no minimum order or subscription attached to it.

It's there for anyone, but it makes the biggest difference if you're housebound, caring for someone, working hours that don't line up with ours, or just without an easy way to get to Malabar Road. If that's you, call us and we'll set it up.

Genuinely free

No delivery charge, no minimum spend, no membership. You'll still pay the standard NHS prescription charge if you're not exempt — that's it.
