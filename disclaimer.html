If this is an emergency

Call 999 immediately if someone is seriously ill or injured, or their life is at risk. For urgent medical help that is not an emergency, call NHS 111 or visit 111.nhs.uk. Do not wait for a reply from this website.

The content on this website is provided for general information about St Matthew's Pharmacy and the services we offer. It is written to be helpful and accurate, but it is necessarily general and cannot take account of your individual circumstances.

2. Not a substitute for professional advice

Nothing on this website is medical, clinical or pharmaceutical advice, and it does not create a professional relationship between you and us. It must not be used to diagnose a condition or to decide on treatment.

Always speak to a qualified healthcare professional — a pharmacist, your GP, or another appropriate clinician — before starting, stopping or changing any treatment, and before acting on anything you read here.

3. Never delay seeking advice

Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you have read on this website. If your symptoms are getting worse, or you are worried, contact a healthcare professional straight away.
