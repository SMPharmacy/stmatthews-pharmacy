Contraception service

You can now start or continue oral contraception, and get emergency contraception, at the pharmacy — without needing a GP appointment.

Book Online ← Back to services

Ongoing oral contraception

Under the NHS pharmacy contraception service, a pharmacist can supply and manage the oral contraceptive pill — both the combined pill and the progestogen-only ("mini") pill. That includes starting it for the first time and continuing a pill you're already taking. The pharmacist will check your blood pressure and ask about your health to make sure the method is suitable and safe for you, all in our private consultation room.

Emergency contraception

Emergency contraception can prevent pregnancy after unprotected sex or if your usual method has failed. It is most effective the sooner it is taken, so don't wait — speak to us as soon as you can. We can provide the emergency contraceptive pill and advise you on your options, confidentially.

Confidential and judgement-free

  • Private consultation room with a door that closes
  • No GP appointment or referral needed
  • Advice on which method might suit you, and on wider sexual health services
  • We'll signpost you to specialist sexual health or contraception clinics where that's the better option
