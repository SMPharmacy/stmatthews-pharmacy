NHS Service

Pharmacy First

Seven common conditions, assessed and treated by a pharmacist — including prescription-only medicines like antibiotics where they're appropriate. No GP appointment. No referral. Just walk in.

See a pharmacist, not a waiting room

Pharmacy First lets community pharmacists treat seven conditions that used to need a GP. We can supply prescription-only medicines under an NHS patient group direction, which means you can walk in with a sore throat and walk out with what you need.

You'll be seen in our private consultation room — a proper room with a door, not a corner of the shop floor. If we can't help, or if what you've got needs a doctor, we'll tell you straight away and make sure you're pointed at the right place.

What it costs

The consultation itself is always free. If you're exempt from prescription charges, any medicine we supply is free too. If you're not exempt, you pay the standard NHS prescription charge — the same as you would at your GP.

What we can treat

The seven conditions

Each has an NHS age range attached. Select a condition to see its symptoms, who it's for and how to book. If you fall outside the age range, we'll point you to your GP instead.

Sinusitis

Blocked, painful sinuses that aren't shifting. We'll assess whether it's viral or needs antibiotics.

Age 12 and over

Sore Throat

We use a clinical scoring system to work out whether antibiotics would actually help, or whether it'll clear on its own.

Age 5 and over

Earache

Acute ear infections in children. We use an otoscope to look inside the ear and treat accordingly.

Age 1 to 17

Infected Insect Bite

A bite that's got worse rather than better — spreading redness, heat or pus. We'll check whether it's infected.

Age 1 and over

Impetigo

The crusty, golden-yellow skin infection that spreads fast, especially in children. Treated with a topical antibiotic.

Age 1 and over

Shingles

The painful, blistering rash on one side of the body. Antivirals work best within 72 hours — come in as soon as you spot it.

Age 18 and over

Urinary Tract Infection

Uncomplicated lower UTIs. We'll assess your symptoms and supply antibiotics if they're warranted.

Women aged 16 to 64

Not on the list?

We can still give advice on almost anything, and we'll always tell you honestly if you need a GP, 111 or A&E.
